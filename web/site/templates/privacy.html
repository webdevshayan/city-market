Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

City Market operates a 100% Halal delivery marketplace that connects customers with certified-Halal shops and independent delivery riders. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you keep over it.

Data we collect

  • Account data — name, phone number and login credentials when you register.
  • Order data — items ordered, order value and delivery address, needed to fulfil your order.
  • Delivery location — rider location during an active delivery, and your address, only while completing that delivery.
  • Usage data — pages viewed and app interactions, to fix problems and improve the service.
  • Support communications — messages you send us about orders or disputes.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

To place and deliver orders, process payments through licensed payment providers, calculate the fixed fees stated at checkout (shop commission, rider payout, flat delivery fee), provide support, prevent fraud, and keep the marketplace compliant with our Halal policy. Legal bases: performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interest in operating a safe marketplace, and compliance with applicable law.

Who we share it with

  • Shops receive the order and address details needed to prepare it.
  • Riders receive the pickup and delivery details needed to complete it.
  • Payment providers process payments and refunds; we never store full card details on our servers.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Retention

Order and payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and dispute purposes; account data is kept while your account is active and deleted after a verified deletion request, except where the law requires longer retention.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, correction of wrong data, deletion of your account, and withdrawal of marketing consent at any time. You can also object to processing or lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. We answer requests within the timelines required by applicable law.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit; sessions use hardened cookies (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict); and all forms and HTMX requests are protected against cross-site request forgery. Access to stored data is limited to what operations and support genuinely need.

Children

City Market is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of digital consent in their jurisdiction.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the apps and on this page. For privacy requests, use the in-app support channel; our direct contact details are published here at launch.
